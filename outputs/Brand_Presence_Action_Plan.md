## Corrections and limits

- Playbook 13 says two certifications in its outcome and About instructions but names four elsewhere. Use the four supplied names consistently; independently verify their current status and dates before changing credential records.
- Playbook 10's earlier Business Profile and personal YouTube suggestions conflict with later decisions. Use playbook 17's online-only approach and playbook 16's focused personal video portfolio.
- “Recruiters only” is a visibility preference, not a confidentiality guarantee. [LinkedIn says complete privacy cannot be guaranteed](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a507508).
- [Google excludes online-only businesses from Business Profile eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en). Revisit only if the actual operating model changes and meets the current requirements.
- Structured data can help explain page content, but [Google does not guarantee enhanced search appearance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies). Do not promise page-one placement or a Knowledge Panel.
- Audience totals, ranking factors, percentage reach penalties, hashtag limits and API prices in the playbooks were not verified here. They are not foundations for this plan. Check official documentation before implementing platform-specific rules.
- The supplied playbooks identify hemayethossain.com, hossainconsulting.com and portfolio.hossainconsulting.com. The web reader could not access them during this review. This does not establish that they are offline; live content, navigation and SEO still need an authenticated or browser review.
- Actual social account settings, handles, credential URLs, booking availability and search-console ownership were not verified. Do not create guessed profile or booking links.

## Sustainable weekly routine

Choose two short sessions for matched job applications and LinkedIn profile work, plus one session to improve and document a project. Publish a useful project update only when there is something demonstrable to share. Repurpose that same evidence for other channels as capacity allows.

Track relevant recruiter enquiries, interview invitations, qualified client conversations and completed project demonstrations. Record actual counts; treat goals as goals, not forecasts.

## Scope of this update

Updated the public résumé repository and prepared the accompanying LinkedIn copy. No social posts or messages were sent, account settings changed, website deployments made, or search-console registrations submitted. Source playbooks were preserved.
